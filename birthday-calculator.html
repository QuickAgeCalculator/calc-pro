How Does the Birthday Calculator Work?

Calculating chronological age accurately requires handling Gregorian calendar complexities. Standard simple subtraction fails because calendar months vary between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days, and leap years occur every four years (excluding century years not divisible by 400).

Our algorithm executes a multi-step calendar validation:

  1. It calculates initial year, month, and day differences between the target date and birth date.
  2. If the target day is less than the birth day, it borrows the exact number of days in the previous month.
  3. If the target month is less than the birth month, it borrows 12 months from the target year.
  4. It verifies whether February 29 leap days fall within the evaluated range, ensuring complete mathematical precision.
