How to Convert Marks to Percentage?

Understanding the fundamental mathematical formula used worldwide by schools and universities.

Basic Formula

Percentage (%) = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Step-by-Step Calculation

  1. Sum the Marks Obtained: Calculate the total aggregate marks scored across all your subjects.
  2. Sum the Maximum Marks: Determine the maximum potential aggregate total (for example, 5 subjects of 100 marks each equals 500 total).
  3. Divide: Divide your total obtained marks by the maximum total marks.
  4. Multiply by 100: Multiply the decimal result by 100 to yield your final overall percentage.

Example 1 – Single Subject

If a student scores 82 marks out of 100 in Mathematics:
• Calculation: (82 ÷ 100) × 100
• Result = 82%

Example 2 – Multiple Subjects

If a student scores 425 marks out of 500 total in 5 subjects:
• Calculation: (425 ÷ 500) × 100
• Result = 85%

How to Calculate Percentage from Marks of Multiple Subjects?

Detailed guide for calculating overall aggregate percentage across 5 to 6 subjects.

Formula for Overall Percentage

When dealing with multiple subjects, do not average individual subject percentages unless all subjects share the exact same maximum mark. Instead, compute the sum of all obtained marks and divide by the sum of all maximum marks:

Overall Percentage = (Sum of Obtained Marks in All Subjects ÷ Total Maximum Marks of All Subjects) × 100
