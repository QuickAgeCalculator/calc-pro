How Age Difference is Calculated

Calculating the exact age difference between two people requires more than simple subtraction because month lengths vary between 28 and 31 days and leap years introduce extra calendar days every four years.

Age Gap Formula: | Birth Date 1 − Birth Date 2 |
Full Years = Floor(Difference in years)
Remaining Months = Calendar months remaining after full years
Remaining Days = Adjusting for exact calendar days in the final month

Step-by-Step Worked Math Example

Consider comparing January 15, 1990 and March 20, 1995:

  • Full Years: From Jan 15, 1990 to Jan 15, 1995 = 5 complete years.
  • Remaining Months: From Jan 15, 1995 to March 15, 1995 = 2 complete months.
  • Remaining Days: From March 15, 1995 to March 20, 1995 = 5 days.
  • Final Answer: Exactly 5 years, 2 months, and 5 days.

Half Your Age Plus 7 Rule Explained

The "Half Your Age Plus Seven" rule is a well-known social benchmark used to determine the minimum socially acceptable age for a dating partner. It posits that a person should never date someone younger than half their age plus seven years.

Minimum Partner Age Formula
Min Age = (Your Age ÷ 2) + 7
Maximum Partner Age Formula
Max Age = (Your Age − 7) × 2
