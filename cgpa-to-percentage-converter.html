How to Convert CGPA to Percentage?

Converting Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) into an equivalent percentage requires applying specific conversion factors determined by your educational board or university standard. Below are the official formulas for various scales and institutions.

10

CGPA to Percentage Formula (10 Point Scale)

Used by CBSE, AICTE, UGC, and major Indian Central Universities.

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
4

CGPA to Percentage Formula (4 Point Scale)

Common in the US, Canada, and international institutions.

Percentage = (GPA / 4.0) × 100
5

CGPA to Percentage Formula (5 Point Scale)

Utilized by European and selective Commonwealth universities.

Percentage = (CGPA / 5.0) × 100

University-wise CGPA to Percentage Conversion Methods

While the 9.5 multiplier recommended by CBSE is widespread, several technical universities follow custom conversion rules.

CBSE / AICTE / UGC

Standard formula across India

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

VTU (Visvesvaraya Technological Univ)

Official Engineering formula

Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

Mumbai University

10-Point Choice Based Credit Standard

Percentage = (CGPA × 7.1) + 11

Anna University

Direct 10 multiplier standard

Percentage = CGPA × 10

University of Delhi (DU)

UGC Choice Based Credit System

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

KTU (Kerala Technological Univ)

Technical degree conversion formula

Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) × 10

How to Convert Percentage to CGPA?

When converting overall percentage marks back to Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA), you invert the standard university multiplier.

Percentage to CGPA Formula (10 Point Scale)

CGPA = Percentage / 9.5

Divide your percentage by 9.5 to obtain your equivalent grade point average on a 10-point scale.

Step-by-Step Example

Target: Convert 85.5% to CGPA.

Step 1: Identify percentage = 85.5%.

Step 2: Divide by multiplier 9.5.

85.5 ÷ 9.5 = 9.0 CGPA

Difference Between CGPA and Percentage

While both evaluate academic achievements, CGPA and Percentage differ significantly in structure and measurement methodology.

Feature CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) Percentage (%)
Definition Average grade point obtained across all completed courses/semesters. Total marks obtained divided by maximum total marks multiplied by 100.
Scale Usually measured on a 10.0, 4.0, or 5.0 scale. Measured on a 0 to 100 scale.
Granularity Groups student scores into grade point bands. Reflects exact individual marks score.
Global Recognition Widely adopted globally for international higher education. Commonly used in traditional competitive exams and job postings.
