How to Convert SGPA to CGPA?

Understanding the formulas used by academic evaluation systems.

Credit-Weighted Formula Recommended

Most colleges assign different weightage (credits) to each semester. This is the official and most accurate method.

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Simple Average Formula

Used only when every semester carries an exact equal credit weightage across your entire degree course.

CGPA = (SGPA₁ + SGPA₂ + … + SGPAₙ) ÷ N

Difference Between SGPA and CGPA

Both metrics evaluate academic grades, but they serve different timeframes.

What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) reflects the grade earned by a student in a single specific semester. It is derived from the grade points of all subjects taken during that semester.

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) measures cumulative academic achievement across all completed semesters up to the current point in time.

Feature / Aspect SGPA CGPA
Full Form Semester Grade Point Average Cumulative Grade Point Average
Scope Single semester performance Overall course performance (all semesters)
Fluctuates High variability from semester to semester Stabilizes over time as total credits increase
Usage Term report cards & mid-course evaluations Final degree classification, job placement & higher studies

How to Calculate CGPA from SGPA – Detailed Guide

Explore the exact mathematical procedures step-by-step.

Method 1: Using Credits (Official University Method)

The credit-weighted system gives higher weight to semesters with higher total credit hours (such as semesters containing heavy lab work or major projects).

  1. List down each semester's SGPA and assigned total credit hours.
  2. Multiply each semester's SGPA by its credit value.
  3. Add up all the resulting numbers to obtain total weighted quality points.
  4. Divide this total sum by the grand total of credits across all listed semesters.

Method 2: Simple Average

If every semester in your program carries identical credit points (for instance, exactly 20 credits every semester), you can simply average the SGPAs:

CGPA = (SGPA₁ + SGPA₂ + SGPA₃ + SGPA₄) ÷ 4

Which Method Should You Use?

Always default to Method 1 (Credit-Weighted) because almost all technical and non-technical universities (including VTU, Anna University, AKTU, SPPU, Mumbai University) mandate credit-weighted CGPA evaluation.

University-wise Notes on SGPA to CGPA

Specific guidelines for top Indian universities.

VTU SGPA to CGPA

VTU uses CBCS scheme with strict credit weighting. To convert VTU CGPA to percentage, the standard VTU formula is: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10.

Anna University

Follows official credit-weighted CGPA. For conversion to equivalent percentage, Anna University prescribes: Percentage = CGPA × 10.

AKTU / SPPU / Others

AKTU and SPPU calculate CGPA using credit weighting. AICTE guideline multiplier for standard percentage conversion is CGPA × 9.5.
