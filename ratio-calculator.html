What is a Ratio?

A ratio is a mathematical comparison of two or more numbers showing their relative sizes to one another. Understanding ratio meaning is crucial across mathematics, financial analysis, cooking, architecture, and everyday problem solving. When asking what is ratio, it represents how many times one number contains another.

For instance, if a bowl contains 8 apples and 12 oranges, the ratio of apples to oranges is written as 8 : 12, which simplifies down to 2 : 3.

Key Distinction: Ratio vs Proportion

While a ratio compares two standalone quantities (e.g., 2 : 3), a proportion is an equation asserting that two ratios are exactly equivalent (e.g., 2 : 3 = 8 : 12).

Formula & How It Works

1. Simplification Formula (GCD)

To reduce a ratio A : B to its simplest whole number form, divide both terms by their Greatest Common Divisor (GCD):

Asimplified = A / GCD(A, B)   |   Bsimplified = B / GCD(A, B)

2. Cross-Multiplication for Proportions

When solving for an unknown variable x in an equation such as A / B = C / x:

A × x = B × C  &implies;  x = (B × C) / A

3. Scaling Formula

To scale a ratio A : B by a multiplier factor k:

Ascaled = A × k   |   Bscaled = B × k

Worked Examples

Example 1: Simplification

Simplify 24 : 36

  1. Find GCD of 24 and 36 → GCD = 12
  2. Divide 24 by 12 = 2
  3. Divide 36 by 12 = 3
  4. Final Simplified Ratio = 2 : 3
Example 2: Proportion Solver

Solve 2 : 3 = 8 : x

  1. Cross-multiply diagonally: 2 × x = 3 × 8
  2. 2 × x = 24
  3. Divide by 2: x = 24 / 2
  4. Final Answer x = 12
Example 3: Scaling Ratio

Scale 3 : 5 by factor 4

  1. Multiply first term: 3 × 4 = 12
  2. Multiply second term: 5 × 4 = 20
  3. Scaled Ratio = 12 : 20
Example 4: Recipe Scaling

Recipe Flour : Sugar = 3 : 1

  1. If you use 9 cups of flour, find sugar required.
  2. Proportion: 3 / 1 = 9 / x
  3. 3x = 9 → x = 3 cups of sugar.
  4. Adjusted Recipe = 9 cups Flour : 3 cups Sugar
