What is Exact Age & How Age is Calculated

Your exact age represents the complete span of your lifespan measured from the moment of birth. In standard calendar conventions, chronological age advances on the exact anniversary of your birthday.

Age Calculation Formula & Leap Year Handling

When using a free age calculator with leap year support, the calculation algorithm evaluates leap years (years divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400). For instance, February contains 29 days in a leap year instead of 28. If you were born on February 29, the algorithm calculates exact non-leap year anniversaries on March 1st or February 28th according to international legal standards.

Formula Overview:
Current Date − Birth Date = Chronological Age (Years, Remaining Months, Remaining Days)

Example Calculation: If a person was born on 15 June 1990 and you calculate their age as of 26 September 2026:

  • Year difference: 2026 − 1990 = 36 Years
  • Month difference: September (9) − June (6) = 3 Months
  • Day difference: 26 − 15 = 11 Days
  • Exact Calculated Age: 36 Years, 3 Months, and 11 Days.
