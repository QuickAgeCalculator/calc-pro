What is Exact Age & How Age is Calculated

Chronological age refers to the exact time passed from the day of a person's birth to a specified target date. While it sounds simple, manual age calculation can be tricky because calendar months vary between 28, 30, and 31 days, and leap years occur every four years (adding February 29th).

Formula Overview:
Current Date − Birth Date = Chronological Age (Years, Remaining Months, Remaining Days)

Example Calculation: If a person was born on 15 June 1990 and you calculate their age as of 26 September 2026:

  • Year difference: 2026 − 1990 = 36 Years
  • Month difference: September (9) − June (6) = 3 Months
  • Day difference: 26 − 15 = 11 Days
  • Exact Calculated Age: 36 Years, 3 Months, and 11 Days.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Doing manual date subtraction is prone to human error due to leap years (366 days) and varying lengths of months (28, 30, or 31 days).

Official government forms, job applications, school admissions cutoff dates, insurance policies, and retirement planning demand 100% exact chronological age. Our free age calculator guarantees instant accuracy without manual math.

Age Calculation Formula

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

1. Year Calculation: Initial Years = Current Year − Birth Year.
2. Month Adjustment: If the current month is earlier than the birth month, subtract 1 from Years and add 12 to Months.
3. Day Adjustment: If the current day is smaller than the birth day, subtract 1 from Months and add total days of the preceding month.

How Old Am I in Different Units?

Age can be represented in various standard time units depending on context. Here is how your calculated age breaks down:

Age in Years Standard solar orbit count
Age in Months Years × 12 + remaining months
Age in Weeks Total days divided by 7
Age in Days Total cumulative days lived
Age in Hours Total days × 24 hours
Age in Minutes Total hours × 60 minutes
