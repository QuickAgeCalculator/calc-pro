How to Convert SGPA to Percentage?

Converting your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) to an aggregate percentage is a common requirement during job recruitment drives (like TCS, Wipro, Infosys), higher education admissions (GATE, CAT, GRE), and visa application processes.

To manually convert SGPA to Percentage, you must apply the official conversion multiplier assigned by your university or follow the UGC (University Grants Commission) standard guidelines. The primary conversion method involves multiplying the grade point by a fixed factor (typically 9.5 or 10) or deducting a baseline index (0.75) before multiplying by 10.

SGPA to Percentage Conversion Formula

Most Common UGC Formula

Percentage = (SGPA - 0.75) × 10

Officially recommended by UGC and adopted by prominent central and state universities across India. Subtract 0.75 from your total SGPA score and multiply the remainder by 10.

Direct Multiplier Formula

Percentage = SGPA × 9.5

Widely used by CBSE, VTU (Visvesvaraya Technological University), Anna University, and many technical institutes following a 10-point scale.

AKTU / SPPU / JNTUH Style

Percentage = (SGPA × 10) - 7.5

Popularly utilized in Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), and JNTU Hyderabad engineering degree curriculums.

Mumbai University Formula

Percentage = (SGPA × 7.1) + 11

Mumbai University relies on a unique linear regression grading system for engineering and commerce streams under CBGS guidelines.

Difference Between SGPA and CGPA

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

  • Measures academic performance in a single semester.
  • Calculated based only on course credits taken in that semester.
  • Helps monitor short-term academic progression.

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

  • Measures overall academic performance across all semesters.
  • Calculated taking weighted averages of all completed course credits.
  • Printed on degree certificates and used for final class distinctions.

SGPA to Percentage Conversion Examples

Example 1: Convert 8.5 SGPA to Percentage

Using standard multiplier formula: 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%.
Using UGC standard formula: (8.5 - 0.75) × 10 = 77.50%.

Example 2: Convert 7.5 SGPA to Percentage

Using standard multiplier formula: 7.5 × 9.5 = 71.25%.
Using UGC standard formula: (7.5 - 0.75) × 10 = 67.50%.
