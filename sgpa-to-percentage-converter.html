How to Convert SGPA to Percentage?

Converting your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) to a percentage depends on the conversion formula approved by your university academic board.

1

Most Common Formula

Used by VTU, AICTE, and majority of technical universities.

Percentage = (SGPA − 0.75) × 10

Alternatively written as: Percentage = (SGPA × 10) − 7.5

2

Direct Multiplier Formula

Used by Anna University, Bharathiar University & others.

Percentage = SGPA × 10

Direct 1:10 scaling without deducting offset points.

3

CBSE / Delhi University Style

Adopted by DU, CBSE, and several central universities.

Percentage = SGPA × 9.5

Multiplies grade point directly by 9.5 factor.

4

JNTUH / GTU / Mumbai University

State specific technical board standards.

JNTUH/GTU: (SGPA − 0.5) × 10
Mumbai Univ: (7.25 × SGPA) + 11

Tailored for credit weight distributions.

University-wise SGPA to Percentage Conversion Rules

Detailed review of official guidelines issued by prominent Indian universities for grade conversions.

VTU SGPA to Percentage

Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) uses the official formula Percentage = (SGPA - 0.75) × 10. Applicable for all BE/B.Tech CBCS schemes.

Anna University SGPA

Anna University follows a direct multiplication method where Percentage = SGPA × 10 for smooth equivalency evaluations.

AKTU / SPPU / MAKAUT

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University and Savitribai Phule Pune University prescribe Percentage = (SGPA × 10) - 7.5.

Mumbai University

Mumbai University adopts Percentage = (7.25 × SGPA) + 11 for Engineering streams under CBCGS guidelines.

JNTUH & GTU

Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad and Gujarat Technological University use (SGPA - 0.5) × 10.

Calcutta & RGPV

RGPV Bhopal and Calcutta University follow the standard AICTE formula (SGPA - 0.75) × 10 for 10-point scale grading.

How to Convert Percentage to SGPA?

To convert marks percentage back into standard SGPA on a 10-point scale, reverse your university's official formula.

Reverse Standard Formula

SGPA = (Percentage / 10) + 0.75

Example for 77.5%: (77.5 / 10) + 0.75 = 7.75 + 0.75 = 8.50 SGPA.

Reverse Direct Formula (Anna Univ)

SGPA = Percentage / 10

Example for 85%: 85 / 10 = 8.50 SGPA.

Difference Between SGPA and CGPA

Understanding academic evaluation metrics used in university mark sheets.

Feature SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)
Scope Measures academic performance in a single semester. Measures cumulative performance across all completed semesters.
Calculation Basis Total grade points earned in subjects divided by total credits in that semester. Weighted sum of all SGPA credit points divided by total credits earned till date.
When to Use For single semester review, re-evaluation, or semester rank list. For final degree classification, placements, higher studies, and resume listing.
