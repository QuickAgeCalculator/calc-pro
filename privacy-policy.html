Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026
Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Calculator Pro (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We operate the website calculatorpro.com which provides free online calculator tools for everyday calculations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using Calculator Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Your calculator data never leaves your browser. All calculations are performed locally on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any numbers or values you enter into our calculators.

2.1 Calculator Inputs

Any data you type into our calculators (such as numbers, amounts, measurements, dates, etc.) is processed entirely within your browser using JavaScript. This data:

  • Is never sent to our servers
  • Is never stored by us
  • Is discarded when you leave the page or close the browser tab

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you visit Calculator Pro:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Which calculators are used
  • Device type, browser type and version, and operating system
  • IP address (usually anonymized or truncated)
  • Referral source (how you found our website)
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

2.3 Information You Voluntarily Provide

We only collect personal information if you choose to give it to us, for example:

  • Email address and message content when you contact us
  • Email address if you subscribe to our newsletter (if available)

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Names, addresses, or phone numbers through the calculators
  • Financial account numbers, credit card details, or bank information
  • Social Security numbers, tax IDs, or government identification numbers
  • Health data, body measurements, or medical information
  • Any sensitive personal information entered into calculator fields

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve Calculator Pro and its tools
  • To understand which calculators are most useful and decide what new tools to build
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns and improve website performance
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To ensure the security of our website and prevent abuse
  • To display relevant advertisements (if advertising is enabled on the site)

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and understand how the site is used.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential / Functional Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly and to remember your preferences (such as theme or currency selection).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site (for example, Google Analytics or similar privacy-focused tools).
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) to serve relevant ads based on your browsing activity.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You may also manage your preferences through our cookie consent banner (where available).

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to help us operate Calculator Pro. These may include:

  • Google Analytics (or privacy-focused alternatives) – for website analytics
  • Google AdSense or other advertising networks – to display ads
  • Cloudflare or similar services – for security, performance, and hosting
  • Email service providers – to handle contact form submissions

These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies for more details.

6. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal data with third parties except in the following limited cases:

  • With service providers who help us operate the website (under strict contractual obligations)
  • When required by law or to respond to legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Calculator Pro, our users, or the public

7. Data Retention

  • Calculator inputs: Never stored by us.
  • Analytics data: Retained in anonymized or aggregated form, typically for 14 to 26 months.
  • Contact form messages: Kept only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry (usually up to 2 years).

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process, including the use of HTTPS encryption. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable laws), you may have the following rights:

  • Right of Access – Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification – Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure – Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing – Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to Object – Object to certain types of processing
  • Right to Data Portability – Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Right to Opt-Out – Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (CCPA)
  • Right to Withdraw Consent – Where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by applicable law.

10. Children’s Privacy

Calculator Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

If you access Calculator Pro from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be transferred to and processed in a country that may have different data protection laws. We take appropriate safeguards to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of Calculator Pro after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
