What is a Work Day / Business Day?

A Work Day (or Business Day) refers to any official day of the week where commercial operations, corporate activities, banking institutions, and formal labor occur. Unlike standard calendar days, working days specifically exclude rest days such as weekends and official statutory public holidays.

Standard 5-Day Week

Monday to Friday. Typical for Western nations, corporate sectors, and global financial markets.

Middle East 5-Day Week

Sunday to Thursday with Friday and Saturday off, aligned with traditional religious schedules.

6-Day Work Week

Monday to Saturday with Sunday off. Common in retail, manufacturing, and emerging economies.

How the Calculator Works / Formula

Our Work Days Calculator iterates through every single date within your specified timeframe, evaluates whether the day is a designated weekend day or matches an excluded holiday date, and returns the net active work days:

Working Days = Total Calendar Days − Weekend Days − Public Holidays (falling on weekdays)

Excel Equivalents:

• Standard 5-day week: =NETWORKDAYS(start_date, end_date, [holidays])

• Custom weekends: =NETWORKDAYS.INTL(start_date, end_date, weekend, [holidays])

Weekdays vs. Working Days

Weekdays strictly refer to standard calendar days between Monday and Friday, regardless of holidays. Working Days, however, exclude both weekend days AND active public or corporate holidays on which business is suspended.

• Monday – Thursday: Weekday & Working Day
• Friday (Public Holiday): Weekday, but NOT a Working Day
• Saturday & Sunday: Weekend

Work Days vs. Calendar Days

Calendar Days encompass every continuous 24-hour cycle from Sunday through Saturday. Work Days measure effective operational labor days.

Example: 7 Calendar Days → 5 Weekdays → 4 Working Days (if 1 public holiday falls on a weekday).

Are the Start Date and End Date Included?

By default, our calculator uses inclusive counting. This means both the selected Start Date and End Date are evaluated. If your project starts on Monday and completes on Friday of the same week, inclusive counting yields 5 Working Days. If you uncheck "Include End Date", the result adjusts to 4 elapsed work days.
