1 How to Convert SGPA to CGPA?

Converting your Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) to a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is an essential step for university students tracking their academic performance. SGPA reflects a student's scholastic output in a single semester, while CGPA measures cumulative academic performance over all completed semesters.

To convert SGPA to CGPA, you can either compute the simple arithmetic mean of all semester SGPAs or calculate the credit-weighted average if course credit hours vary across terms. Our online SGPA to CGPA Converter automates both methods seamlessly for diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate programs.

2 SGPA to CGPA Conversion Formula

Universities across the world follow two main formulas for CGPA calculation from semester SGPAs under the UGC Choice Based Credit System (CBCS).

Simple Average Formula

Used when every semester carries equal weightage or credit distribution:

CGPA = (Sum of all Semester SGPAs) ÷ (Total Number of Semesters)

Formula expression: CGPA = (SGPA₁ + SGPA₂ + ... + SGPAₙ) / N

Credit-Weighted Formula

Recommended for engineering and credit-based degree courses:

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Semester Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Formula expression: Σ(Sᵢ × Cᵢ) / ΣCᵢ

4 Difference Between SGPA and CGPA

Understanding the distinction between SGPA and CGPA is vital when submitting mark sheets for job placements or higher academic studies:

Comparison Aspect SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)
Scope Evaluates single semester performance Evaluates overall performance across all semesters
Time Duration Short term (usually 6 months) Long term (2 to 4 year degree duration)
Fluctuation High variation between semesters More stable as it averages multiple semesters
Key Purpose Semester mark sheet assessment Final Degree Classification & Placements

5 How to Calculate CGPA from SGPA – Detailed Guide

Follow these three easy steps to compute your overall grade point average using our free online SGPA to CGPA calculator:

Method 1: Using Credits (Official University Method)

The credit-weighted system gives higher weight to semesters with higher total credit hours (such as semesters containing heavy lab work or major projects).

  1. List down each semester's SGPA and assigned total credit hours.
  2. Multiply each semester's SGPA by its credit value.
  3. Add up all the resulting numbers to obtain total weighted quality points.
  4. Divide this total sum by the grand total of credits across all listed semesters.

Method 2: Simple Average

If every semester in your program carries identical credit points (for instance, exactly 20 credits every semester), you can simply average the SGPAs:

CGPA = (SGPA₁ + SGPA₂ + SGPA₃ + SGPA₄) ÷ 4

Which Method Should You Use?

Always default to Method 1 (Credit-Weighted) because almost all technical and non-technical universities (including VTU, Anna University, AKTU, SPPU, Mumbai University) mandate credit-weighted CGPA evaluation.

6 University-wise Notes on SGPA to CGPA

Different technical and non-technical universities follow minor variations in converting SGPA to CGPA or Percentage:

  • Anna University & VTU: Uses credit-weighted SGPA calculation for B.E / B.Tech degrees. Percentage is calculated as (CGPA - 0.75) × 10.
  • AKTU / UPTU & DU: Follows standard UGC 10-point credit scale where overall CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) / Total Credits.
  • Mumbai University & SPPU: Calculates CGPA by taking credit-weighted average of all semesters from 1 to 8.
  • CBSE & Central Universities: Follows standard 10-point grading formula where Percentage = CGPA × 9.5.
