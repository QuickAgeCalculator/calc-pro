GST Calculation Formulas

Exclusive GST (Add GST)

Used when tax percentage needs to be added on top of base amount:

GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) / 100
Total Invoice Price = Base Amount + GST Amount

Inclusive GST (Remove GST)

Used to calculate net base price when tax is already inside total retail price:

Base Amount = Total Price × [100 / (100 + GST Rate)]
GST Tax Amount = Total Price - Base Amount

CGST + SGST (Intra-state Supply)

When buyer and seller are located in the same state:

CGST Amount = Total GST Amount / 2
SGST Amount = Total GST Amount / 2

IGST (Inter-state Supply)

When supply occurs across two different state boundaries:

IGST Amount = Entire 100% Total GST Amount

How to Remove GST from Price

When dealing with MRP or gross invoices where tax is already embedded, a reverse GST calculator is essential. By reversing the tax percentage using our tool's "Remove GST" mode, you extract the exact Input Tax Credit (ITC) or base manufacturing cost without manual division errors.

GST Exclusive vs Inclusive

Exclusive GST means tax is calculated on top of the listed price at checkout (common in B2B transactions). Inclusive GST means the tax is already bundled into the final price paid by the consumer (common in retail MRP).

CGST SGST Calculator Breakdown

For intra-state trades within the same state, tax is split equally between the Central Government (CGST) and State Government (SGST). For inter-state transactions, the total tax is collected as Integrated GST (IGST).
