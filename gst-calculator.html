Latest GST Slabs in India 2026 (GST 2.0)

Under the revised GST 2.0 framework, tax slabs have been streamlined into four simple tiers to simplify tax filing and reduce compliance burdens for businesses.

Current GST Rate Structure

GST Rate Applicable On (Examples)
0% (Nil) Essential food items, life-saving medicines, education, health insurance
5% Daily essentials, packaged food, most medicines, soap, toothpaste, apparel ≤ ₹2500
18% Standard rate – most goods & services, electronics, small cars, restaurants, IT services
40% Luxury & sin goods – aerated drinks, tobacco, pan masala, luxury cars, high-cc bikes

GST Calculation Formulas

Exclusive GST (Add GST)

GST Amount = (Original Amount × GST Rate) / 100
Final Amount = Original Amount + GST Amount

Inclusive GST (Remove GST)

Original Amount = Final Amount × 100 / (100 + GST Rate)
GST Amount = Final Amount – Original Amount

CGST + SGST (Intra-state)

CGST = GST Amount / 2
SGST = GST Amount / 2

IGST (Inter-state)

IGST = Full GST Amount

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

In 2026, under GST 2.0 regulations, India utilizes a four-tier rate structure: 0% for essential non-processed food items; 5% for daily necessities and basic apparel; 18% standard rate for most commercial goods, electronics, and services; and 40% for luxury items and sin goods like aerated beverages and tobacco products.

To calculate GST on an inclusive price, use the formula: Base Amount = (Total Inclusive Price × 100) / (100 + GST Rate). Then subtract the Base Amount from the Total Inclusive Price to obtain the exact GST component included in the retail maximum retail price (MRP).

CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) apply to intra-state transactions occurring within the same state boundaries and are split evenly (50:50). IGST (Integrated GST) applies to inter-state sales across state borders and is collected as a unified central tax before being apportioned.
