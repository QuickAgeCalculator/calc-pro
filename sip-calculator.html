What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP Calculator (Systematic Investment Plan Calculator) is a financial planning tool designed to simulate the future value of monthly mutual fund investments. It helps investors calculate total capital invested, estimated wealth gain, and overall maturity corpus without tedious manual mathematics.

Regular SIP

Fixed monthly contributions made consistently over a designated timeframe.

Step-up SIP

Increases investment annually in tandem with annual salary increments.

Goal-Based SIP

Reverse-engineers monthly contribution needed for major goals like buying a home or retirement.

SIP Calculator Formula Explained

The standard financial formula for calculating future value of a Systematic Investment Plan is:

FV = P × [{(1 + i)n - 1} / i] × (1 + i)
FV: Future Value (Maturity Amount)
P: Monthly Investment Amount
i: Monthly rate of interest (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)

Practical Worked Example:

If you invest ₹10,000 every month for 10 years (120 months) at an expected return of 12% per annum:
• Total Invested Capital = ₹10,000 × 120 = ₹12,00,000
• Estimated Wealth Gain = ₹11,23,391
• Expected Maturity Corpus = ₹23,23,391

Step-up SIP & Inflation-Adjusted Returns

A Step-up SIP allows you to automatically increase your monthly investment by a predetermined percentage (e.g., 10%) each year. Because income typically grows over time, stepping up your SIP drastically reduces the time required to build a ₹1 Crore corpus.

Why Inflation Matters: ₹1 Crore in 20 years will not have the same purchasing power as ₹1 Crore today. If inflation averages 6% annually, ₹1 Crore after 20 years will feel like approximately ₹31.1 Lakhs today. Always use Calculator Pro's Inflation Adjustment Feature to plan for real purchasing power!
