What is a SIP Calculator?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) calculator is a financial tool designed to simulate the potential future value of periodic investments in mutual funds. It removes manual complex compound interest calculations and gives investors a clear roadmap toward achieving financial goals like retirement, education, or buying a home.

Who Should Use It?

Whether you are a beginner starting with ₹500/month or an experienced investor planning a disciplined wealth accumulation strategy, a SIP calculator helps align your monthly savings with real-world financial goals.

Regular SIP

Fixed monthly investment amount kept constant across the entire tenure.

Step-Up SIP

Increases investment annually in step with annual income/salary growth.

Goal-Based SIP

Target-driven investing to reach a specific target corpus (e.g., ₹1 Crore).

SIP Calculator Formula Explained

Mutual fund SIP calculations are based on the compound interest formula adapted for annuities, where interest compounds monthly:

M = P × ({[1 + i]^n - 1} / i) × (1 + i)
M: Final Maturity Amount
P: Monthly SIP Amount
i: Periodic Monthly Return Rate
(Annual Return ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
n: Total Monthly Payments
(Tenure Years × 12)

Step-by-Step Worked Example:

Assuming P = ₹10,000/month, Expected Return = 12% per annum, Tenure = 10 Years (120 months):

  • i = 12 / 12 / 100 = 0.01
  • n = 10 × 12 = 120
  • M = 10,000 × ({[1 + 0.01]^120 - 1} / 0.01) × (1 + 0.01)
  • M = 10,000 × (2.30038) × 1.01 = ₹23,23,391

Benefits of SIP Investing

  • Rupee Cost Averaging: Automatically buys more fund units when prices drop and fewer when markets rise.
  • Disciplined Saving: Automated bank debits enforce consistent monthly financial discipline.
  • Low Barrier to Entry: Start equity market participation with as little as ₹500/month.
  • Flexibility: Modify, pause, or increase investments anytime without penalization.

Step-Up SIP & Inflation Adjusted Returns

As your annual salary or business revenues grow, keeping your SIP contribution static means missing out on compounding momentum. A 10% yearly Step-up SIP can virtually double your ultimate maturity wealth over a 20-year period compared to a flat SIP.

Simultaneously, inflation erodes future purchasing power. A corpus of ₹1 Crore after 20 years will not buy the same goods as ₹1 Crore today. Our calculator's built-in inflation adjustment feature gives you the true "real money value" in today's rupee terms.
