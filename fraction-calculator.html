What is a Fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole number, written as two numbers separated by a fraction line (a/b).

Proper Fraction

Numerator < Denominator

The top number is smaller than the bottom number (e.g. 3/4 or 1/2).

Improper Fraction

Numerator ≥ Denominator

The top number is equal to or larger than the bottom number (e.g. 5/4 or 7/3).

2 ½

Mixed Number

Whole Number + Fraction

Combines a whole integer with a proper fraction (e.g. 2 1/2 or 1 3/4).

½ = ²/₄

Equivalent Fractions

Equal Value

Fractions that look different but share the same overall numerical value (e.g. 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8).

How to Perform Operations on Fractions

Formulas and simple steps for fraction math operations.

How to Add Fractions

To add fractions with identical denominators, simply add the numerators. If the denominators are different, find the Least Common Denominator (LCD) first:

a/b + c/d = (a·d + b·c)/(b·d)

Example: 1/3 + 1/4 = (1×4 + 3×1)/12 = 7/12

How to Subtract Fractions

Convert both fractions to common denominators, then subtract the second numerator from the first:

a/b − c/d = (a·d − b·c)/(b·d)

Example: 3/4 − 1/2 = 3/4 − 2/4 = 1/4

How to Multiply Fractions

Multiply top numbers together and bottom numbers together directly:

a/b × c/d = (a × c)/(b × d)

Example: 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5

How to Divide Fractions (Reciprocal Method)

Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal (flipped version) of the second fraction:

a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c = (a × d)/(b × c)

Example: 1/2 ÷ 3/4 = 1/2 × 4/3 = 4/6 = 2/3

How to Simplify Fractions

Divide both numerator and denominator by their Greatest Common Divisor (GCD):

Simplified = (a ÷ GCD)/(b ÷ GCD)

Example: Reduce 8/12 with GCD = 4 → (8÷4)/(12÷4) = 2/3

Step-by-Step Examples

Real world problem walkthroughs matching calculator results.

Basic Addition

Solve 2/5 + 3/10

  1. LCD of 5 and 10 is 10.
  2. Convert 2/5 to 4/10.
  3. Add numerators: 4 + 3 = 7.
  4. Final Result: 7/10
Mixed Multiplication

Solve 1 1/2 × 2 2/3

  1. Convert to improper: 3/2 × 8/3.
  2. Multiply: (3×8) / (2×3) = 24/6.
  3. Simplify 24 ÷ 6 = 4.
  4. Final Result: 4
Fraction Simplification

Simplify 36/48

  1. Find GCD of 36 and 48 (GCD = 12).
  2. Divide numerator: 36 ÷ 12 = 3.
  3. Divide denominator: 48 ÷ 12 = 4.
  4. Final Result: 3/4
