Trust & Quality Framework

Editorial Guidelines

Last Updated: October 15, 2026
Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1 Our Commitment

At Calculator Pro, we are committed to providing accurate, transparent, and useful free online calculators and supporting content. These Editorial Guidelines explain how we create, verify, maintain, and improve every tool and page on our website.

Our goal is simple: help users understand calculations clearly, see the assumptions involved, and make better-informed decisions — without claiming professional expertise we do not have.

2 Our Mission

Calculator Pro exists to make practical calculations accessible to everyone. We focus on:

  • Building reliable calculator tools that run entirely in the browser
  • Explaining formulas, inputs, and limitations in plain language
  • Keeping content accurate, up-to-date, and free from commercial bias
  • Helping users verify results and understand when professional advice is needed

3 Content Quality Standards

Every calculator page and supporting guide on Calculator Pro must meet the following minimum standards before publication:

  • Clear purpose — What the calculator estimates and who it is for
  • Transparent method — The formula or approach used is explained
  • Visible assumptions — Any defaults, simplifications, or limitations are stated
  • Worked examples — Where helpful, realistic examples are included
  • Practical guidance — Content helps users understand the result, not just produce a number
  • Original content — We do not copy content from other calculator websites
  • Mobile-friendly design — Tools and explanations work well on all devices

We do not publish pages solely for search rankings. Every page must provide genuine value to users.

4 Calculator Accuracy & Verification Process

Accuracy is our highest priority. Every calculator is tested before it is published and reviewed regularly thereafter.

Our verification process includes:

  1. Formula identification — We identify the standard mathematical, scientific, financial, or industry formula used.
  2. Source checking — Formulas are cross-checked against primary or authoritative references.
  3. Test cases — We test common inputs, edge cases, and known reference values.
  4. Unit and rounding checks — Unit conversions and rounding behavior are verified.
  5. Usability review — Inputs, labels, and results are checked for clarity on both desktop and mobile.

Where a calculation depends on official rates, limits, or standards (for example tax brackets or conversion factors), we use the most current publicly available data at the time of review.

5 Sources and References

We prefer primary and authoritative sources whenever possible. Acceptable sources include:

  • Government agencies and official statistical bodies
  • International standards organizations (e.g., NIST, ISO)
  • Peer-reviewed scientific or academic publications
  • Recognised textbooks and professional guidelines
  • Official documentation from relevant authorities

We generally avoid relying solely on other calculator websites or unsourced secondary content. When we reference external material, we aim to link directly to the original source.

6 Transparency and Assumptions

We believe users should understand how a result is produced, not just see the final number. On calculator pages we strive to:

  • State the main formula or method used
  • Explain key assumptions and defaults
  • Highlight important limitations
  • Note when a result is an estimate rather than an exact official figure
  • Remind users that results are for educational and informational purposes only

Where a real-world calculation involves additional factors (fees, taxes, local rules, individual circumstances), we note that the tool is a simplified model.

7 Updates and Corrections Policy

We regularly review and update our calculators and content when:

  • Formulas, rates, limits, or standards change
  • Errors or inaccuracies are identified
  • Clearer explanations or better examples can improve usability
  • User feedback highlights confusion or missing context

When a significant update is made, we update the “Last Updated” or “Last Reviewed” date on the relevant page.

How we handle corrections

  1. Review the report against the formula, sources, and test cases
  2. Correct any confirmed issues promptly
  3. Update the page and note the change where appropriate

8 Editorial Independence

Calculator Pro maintains full editorial independence. Our calculator formulas, results, and educational content are not influenced by advertisers, sponsors, or commercial partners.

If the website displays advertising (for example through Google AdSense), those advertisements do not affect:

  • The mathematical logic of any calculator
  • The content or recommendations on any page
  • Which tools we choose to publish or prioritize

We do not accept payment to alter calculation results or to promote specific products within educational content.

9 What We Do Not Claim

To maintain trust and clarity, we make the following commitments:

  • We do not provide professional financial, tax, legal, medical, engineering, or investment advice
  • We do not claim that calculator results are official, certified, or guaranteed
  • We do not invent professional credentials or claim expert review that has not occurred
  • We do not present estimates as personalized recommendations
  • We do not guarantee that results will match every real-world scenario or official calculation

Users should always verify important results independently and consult qualified professionals when decisions involve significant financial, legal, health, or technical consequences.

10 How to Report an Issue or Suggest an Improvement

We welcome feedback from users. If you notice:

  • A possible calculation error
  • Outdated rates, limits, or information
  • Unclear explanations or missing context
  • A suggestion for a new calculator or improvement

Please contact us with the page URL, the inputs you used, the result you received, and (if possible) the expected result or source. We review all reports carefully.
