What is an Average?

In mathematics and statistics, an average represents a single central value that summarizes a larger set of numerical data. It provides a quick snapshot of where the center of a data group lies, allowing easy comparison between different groups.

Example: If five students score 70, 80, 85, 90, and 100 on an exam, the average score is 85. This single number summarizes the overall performance of the group.

Types of Average Explained

1. Arithmetic Mean (Simple Average)

The arithmetic mean is the most common form of average. It is computed by summing all numbers in a dataset and dividing that total by the quantity of numbers.

Formula: Mean = Sum of Values ÷ Number of Values

When to use: Best for uniform data without extreme outliers (e.g., test scores, daily temperature averages).

2. Median

The median is the exact middle value when a dataset is arranged in ascending order. If the dataset has an even count of values, the median is the mean of the two central numbers.

When to use: Ideal when data contains extreme outliers or skewed distributions (e.g., real estate prices, household incomes).

3. Mode

The mode is the number that appears most frequently within a dataset. A dataset can have one mode (unimodal), multiple modes (multimodal), or no mode at all.

When to use: Best for categorical data or discovering popular choices (e.g., most purchased shoe size, top survey response).

4. Weighted Average

A weighted average assigns varying levels of importance (weights) to individual values in the dataset before calculating the overall mean.

Formula: Weighted Average = Σ (Value × Weight) ÷ Σ Weights

When to use: Academic grade point averages (GPA), investment portfolio returns, inventory valuation.

5. Geometric Mean & Harmonic Mean (Advanced)

Geometric Mean: Calculated by taking the nth root of the product of n numbers. Used for exponential growth rates and financial returns.
Harmonic Mean: Calculated as the reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of reciprocals. Used for ratios and speeds.

Average Formula

The fundamental mathematical formula for calculating the arithmetic average is straightforward:

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values
Average = (x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + ... + xₙ) / n
x₁, x₂, x₃ ... = individual values in the sample
n = total quantity of values
